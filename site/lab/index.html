    How the numbers are made
    Placement
    Across: Crippen logP (RDKit). Depth: QED. Colour: first ATC letter. Height: log10 of the legacy accumulation expression (mg input convention), clamped to the stored population’s 1st–99th percentile viewport. Missing inputs stay on the floor. Size uses the legacy log-product score; small offsets separate overlaps. Neither encoding represents safety.
    Formulas
    The mg expression is not concentration or unbound exposure. The log-product uses numeric lethal dose (mg/kg) and active dose (mg/day): its dimensional inputs make it unit-dependent, and its endpoints are incompatible. It is not a therapeutic index or safety ranking. CSV retains historical field names with an explicit derived_status explanation; raw inputs and citations are preserved.
