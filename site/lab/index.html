The drug bench

Each sphere is one drug given one way. This bench preserves route-specific source inputs and your annotations. Therapeutic index and free concentration are not calculated: the archived endpoints are not comparable, and the exposure expression does not establish a concentration.

You can record a value and its source, then export your edits. All records remain on a flat plane; vertical position has no exposure meaning. QED is a computed drug-likeness descriptor, not measured toxicity or safety.

    How the numbers are made
    Placement
    Across: Crippen logP (RDKit). Depth: QED. Colour: first ATC letter. Vertical position and small offsets separate overlapping records only; they do not represent exposure.
    Formulas
    No derived TI or exposure calculation is applied. Their CSV fields are blank and accompanied by a withholding reason; raw input values and source citations are preserved.
