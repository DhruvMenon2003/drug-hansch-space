The drug bench

Most of the drugs in this dataset are missing something. A label gives the dose but not the half-life; a rat study gives a lethal dose but nobody wrote down the oral bioavailability. Each sphere below is one drug given one way. The ones lying on the floor are the ones we can't yet place, because their steady-state concentration can't be computed without all four numbers.

If you have the missing value from a paper, type it in. The drug lifts off the floor to where it belongs.
